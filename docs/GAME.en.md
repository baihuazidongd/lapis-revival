# Game Overview · Lapis Re:LiGHTs (Jewel Fantasy: Radiance Restored / Lapis Re:Lights ~In This World, Idols Use Magic~)

## What kind of game this is

`Lapis Re:LiGHTs` is the mobile game inside a multimedia project (game, anime, music, novels) led by KADOKAWA and
developed and operated by KLab. The player takes on the role of a **teacher (先生／老师)** at an academy that trains idol
witches, leading a group of girls who "sing with magic" onto the stage.

Its worldview carries one beautiful piece of setting: **magic takes song as its medium**. Skills in battle are "chants," and performance and
battle share the same expressive logic — which is why a card's "skill name" sounds like a track title and the battle animation plays like a clip from a concert.

Shutdown date: 2022-10-31. After the Japanese service ended, the content of the work existed only inside the installed client and CDN caches
—— which is exactly why this revival project exists.

## Core systems

| System | Gameplay | Status in this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main story (メインストーリー) | Advances by act and by episode; each episode is "dialogue scene + battle," with a recap of what came before | Playable from start to finish |
| Cards (カード) | Obtain witch cards, level them up, bloom (awaken) them, raise their stars, and load skills and support cards | Available |
| Summon (ガチャ) | Single pull / ten pull, with banners carrying a pity floor and published rates | Available (`P1024`) |
| Stage battles | Turn-based skill combat, auto / speed-up, damage and healing tallies | Available, story stages included |
| Shop (購買部) | Five pages — Radiant Stones / pass / gift bundles / items / exchange counter — with daily limited stock | Available, including billing deduction and delivery persistence |
| Arena (アリーナ) | Asynchronous PVP team matchups with points and rank tiers | Available (`P200`/`P204`) |
| Cheer club (応援会) | Guild / circle system, mutual-aid tasks | Incomplete |
| Dress-up (フィットルーム) | Outfits, weapon looks, frames, display in the club room | Available and persistent |
| Affection / voice | Touch interaction, affinity stories, a large volume of character voice | Partially available |
| Music performance | 3D Live, songs and subtitles | Assets depend on the CDN |

## Why it is worth reviving

After the shutdown, a work with four years' worth of content, thousands of lines of story dialogue, tens of thousands of voice clips and a complete
music library is, technically, nothing more than "that install package on someone's phone." The survival of this kind of work can rest only on local
interoperability research: replacing the parts of the logic that depend on the server with auditable local implementations, so that the content becomes accessible again. This project does not attempt to reopen any service, and does not distribute any game content.
